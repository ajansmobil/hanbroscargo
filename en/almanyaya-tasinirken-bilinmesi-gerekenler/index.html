What should you know before moving to Germany?

Three things matter most when moving to Germany: meeting the customs conditions so your belongings can enter tax-free as removal goods (Übersiedlungsgut), registering your address (Anmeldung) on time once you arrive, and planning what to take around the way German homes are let. This guide brings together everything from shipping your belongings to your first weeks in the country.

Customs: relief for removal goods

Germany is an EU member state, so household goods arriving from Turkey are subject to EU customs rules. EU law allows people moving their normal residence into the EU from outside to bring their personal property without import duty or import VAT, provided certain conditions are met. In Germany these goods are called Übersiedlungsgut, and the procedure is handled by German customs (Zoll). The general conditions are:

  • Residence outside the EU: you must have lived outside the EU for at least 12 consecutive months before moving.
  • Period of use: you are expected to have used the goods for at least six months before the move.
  • Time limit: the goods should generally be declared within 12 months of taking up residence in Germany.
  • No disposal: goods imported under relief must not be sold, hired out or transferred for a set period.

Alcohol, tobacco, commercial vehicles and equipment for professional use are generally excluded, and newly bought items are assessed separately because they do not meet the use condition. Conditions can vary with your circumstances, so confirm the current rules on the official Zoll website and, for export from Turkey, with the Turkish Ministry of Trade.

Documents to prepare

  • Your passport and the visa or residence permit that allows you to live in Germany
  • Evidence that your residence in Turkey has ended
  • A tenancy agreement or registration certificate showing your German address
  • A detailed, numbered inventory of your belongings
  • The declaration form customs requires for removal goods

Check in advance with your removals company's customs team which language and format each document needs to be in.

After arrival: Anmeldung (address registration)

Everyone who moves into a new address in Germany must register it with the local registration office (Bürgeramt or Einwohnermeldeamt). This should generally be done within two weeks of moving in. You will need a confirmation from your landlord (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung). The registration certificate is needed to open a bank account, receive a tax ID and complete many other official tasks. Appointments can be scarce, so try to book as soon as your moving date is fixed.

Planning your home and belongings

Rented flats in Germany can differ from what you are used to in Turkey. Before deciding what to ship, check the following:

  • Kitchen: some rented flats in Germany come without kitchen units or appliances. Ask whether the listing includes an “Einbauküche” (fitted kitchen).
  • Lighting: some flats have no ceiling lights, so taking your lamps and light fittings can make sense.
  • Sockets and power: Germany uses the same plug type as Turkey (Type F), so your appliances will work without adaptors.
  • Cellar storage: many buildings include a cellar compartment (Keller) for each flat; factor this in when planning space for seasonal items.
  • Lift: older buildings may not have a lift, which matters for delivery planning and your quote.

Until you find a home: temporary housing and storage

Finding a suitable rented flat in Germany's larger cities can take time. Many people spend their first weeks in a furnished temporary flat (möbliert) and search for a permanent home locally. In that case, you can time your shipment in one of two ways:

  • Ship once you have a permanent home: your belongings wait in Turkey, ready to pack, and leave once your tenancy is signed. This avoids paying twice for handling but may mean a few weeks' wait after you move in.
  • Ship early and store: your goods leave on your moving date and are held in storage until the delivery address is confirmed. Moving into the permanent home is quicker, but the storage period and insurance cover need to be planned.

Which option suits you depends on how long your search may take, your budget and the time limit for customs relief. Because the removal goods declaration may require proof of residence in Germany, it is best to plan this with the customs team.

Tips for delivery day

In busy German city centres it is not always easy for a removal vehicle to stop outside the building. Many municipalities allow a temporary no-parking zone (Halteverbotszone) to be set up for moving day. The permit must be obtained from the local authority in advance; check the details with the city you are moving to. Your building may also have house rules (Hausordnung) about moving times, so speak to your landlord or property manager beforehand.

Your first-weeks checklist

  • Book your Anmeldung appointment and complete registration
  • Open a bank account
  • Arrange health insurance (health insurance is compulsory in Germany)
  • Check your registration for the household broadcasting fee (Rundfunkbeitrag)
  • Set up electricity, internet and phone contracts
  • Apply for school or nursery places for your children

Bringing your car?

Your private car may also be imported as removal goods, provided it meets the ownership and use conditions. Registering it in Germany (Zulassung) and completing technical approval after customs is a separate process. For more on shipping a vehicle, see our Vehicle Shipping page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I pay tax on household goods taken to Germany?

If you meet the conditions for removal goods (Übersiedlungsgut), your used household belongings can enter without import duty or import VAT. Confirm the conditions on the official Zoll website.

Can I receive my belongings before completing Anmeldung?

Delivery is made to an address and does not depend on registration, but customs may ask for documents showing your residence in Germany. Agree the paperwork with your removals company in advance.

Should I take my kitchen units from Turkey?

If your new flat has no kitchen, it may be worthwhile, but check that the sizes will fit. Shipping units into a flat with a fitted kitchen just adds volume.

Do you deliver to every city in Germany?

Yes. We deliver door to door to Berlin, Hamburg, Munich, Cologne, Frankfurt and every other city in Germany.

Will my white goods work in Germany?

Germany uses the same plug type as Turkey, so your appliances will work without adaptors. Still, check which appliances your new flat already has to avoid shipping unnecessary volume.

Should I choose a part load or a full load?

It depends on your volume and how flexible your dates are. Work out your volume with the Smart Calculation tool and share it with us, and we will compare both options for you.
