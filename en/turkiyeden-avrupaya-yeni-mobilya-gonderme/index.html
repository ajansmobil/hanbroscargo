Sending New Furniture from Turkey to Europe and the UK: What to Know

How do you ship new furniture from Turkey to Europe?

New furniture bought in Turkey is collected from the manufacturer, its packaging is checked, and it then travels as a part load or a full load to your address in Europe or the UK. Because new goods generally fall outside household removal reliefs, a correct invoice and customs declaration are the most important parts of the process.

Why buy furniture in Turkey?

With furniture hubs such as İnegöl in Bursa and the trade centres of Istanbul, Turkey offers a wide choice of styles, made-to-measure production and designs suited to Turkish family life. Many families living in Europe and the UK choose to buy their furniture in Turkey when they marry, move or redecorate. Getting it safely from the showroom or factory to your door, however, takes specialist handling.

What to agree when you buy

Shipping really starts the moment you place your order. Agree the following with the seller:

  • Ready date: get the factory dispatch date in writing; transport is planned around it.
  • Packaging: ask whether the furniture will be supplied in strong, export-grade packaging.
  • Flat-packed parts: find out which pieces will arrive dismantled and whether assembly is needed.
  • Sizes and weights: ask for the packed dimensions and weight of each piece; volume and price depend on them.
  • Invoice: make sure the invoice clearly states the description, quantity and value of each item, as the customs declaration is based on it.
  • Your new home: check door widths, stairwells and lift sizes; a corner sofa that will not fit through the door is a common problem.

From factory to front door, step by step

  1. Collection: furniture is collected directly from the manufacturer or showroom. If you have bought from several sellers, collections can be combined into one shipment.
  2. Checks and records: packaging is inspected on collection and each piece is added to the inventory. Items with inadequate or damaged packaging can be re-packed.
  3. Protective packing: corner protectors, foam wrap and, where needed, a wooden crate prepare the furniture for the journey.
  4. Customs preparation: export and import declarations are prepared from the invoice and inventory.
  5. Transport: depending on volume, the furniture travels as a part load or a full load and is secured so pieces cannot damage each other.
  6. Delivery and assembly: at the destination, pieces are placed in the rooms you choose, with optional assembly and packaging removal.

Customs: why new furniture is different

Customs reliefs for people moving home (ToR1 in the UK, relief for personal property on a change of residence in EU countries) generally apply to goods you have owned and used for a set period. New furniture does not meet that condition, so in most cases it is treated as a taxable import. In practice:

  • Shipping to the EU: under the Turkey–EU Customs Union, industrial goods may benefit from duty-free treatment with an A.TR movement certificate, but VAT in the destination country is usually payable.
  • Shipping to the UK: under the UK–Turkey trade agreement, goods that meet the rules of origin may qualify for preferential duty rates; import VAT is assessed separately.
  • Combined with a household move: if new furniture travels with your used household goods, it must be listed separately on the inventory and declaration.

Duty, VAT and the paperwork required depend on the product, its value and the destination. Confirm the current rules with the Turkish Ministry of Trade, the destination country's customs authority or, for the UK, HMRC on gov.uk; our customs team will guide you when preparing your quote.

Made-to-measure furniture: what to watch

Much of the furniture bought in Turkey is made to order. That makes timing and quality checks even more important:

  • Add production time to your plan: the seller's production lead time and the transit time together decide when your new home will be furnished. Leave some margin for delays.
  • Have it checked at the factory: a wrong colour, fabric, size or finish is far easier to fix while the item is still in Turkey. Ask for photos or a video before packing and, if possible, have someone you trust see it in person.
  • Ask for spare parts and accessories: fixings, legs, handles and spare fabric should travel with the main package, clearly labelled.
  • Keep all paperwork: the invoice, any guarantee and the assembly instructions are needed for customs and after delivery.

A manufacturing fault discovered on arrival may mean sending the item back to Turkey, which is the hardest scenario in terms of time and cost. Moving the quality check to before collection reduces that risk.

Tips for damage-free delivery

  • Ask the seller for photos of the furniture before it leaves the factory.
  • Request extra protection or a crate for pieces with glass or marble surfaces.
  • Make sure upholstered items are packed to keep moisture out.
  • On delivery, check each piece against the inventory, note any visible damage on the delivery paperwork and take photos.
  • Confirm in writing how insurance cover is calculated on the invoice value of new goods.

Getting ready for delivery day

A few simple preparations on delivery day shorten the job and reduce the risk of damage. Clear the route the furniture will take, protect floors and door frames with covers if needed, and decide in advance which room each piece goes in. Arrange where the vehicle will stop outside the building and, if required, a parking permit. If you have booked assembly, check the wall type and socket positions for anything that will be fixed to the wall. On delivery, inspect the outer packaging together before unpacking; if you suspect damage, open the package while the crew is still there.

Which countries do we deliver to?

Han Bros Cargo ships new furniture to the UK, Germany, the Netherlands, France, Belgium, Luxembourg and Austria. You can read more about the scope of the service on our New Furniture Shipping page.

What affects the price?

The price depends on the packed volume and weight of the furniture, the number of collection addresses, the route, whether you choose a part load or a full load, any extra packaging, floor level and access at the delivery address, and additional services such as assembly. The final figure is confirmed in a written quote once the details are known.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can furniture from different shops be sent together?

Yes. With planned collections, items from different sellers can be combined into a single shipment.

Will I pay customs duty on new furniture?

New goods usually fall outside household removal reliefs. Depending on the destination and the origin of the goods, duty and VAT may apply; confirm the current rules with official sources.

Will my furniture be assembled on delivery?

Optional assembly is available for flat-packed furniture. Simply mention it when you request your quote.

My furniture is ready but my home is not. What can I do?

Temporary storage can be arranged where needed. Tell us your delivery date and we will plan around it.

What happens if an item arrives at collection with damaged packaging?

Items with inadequate or damaged packaging are recorded on collection and can be re-packed, and we let you know.
